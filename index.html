The problem

You're not a designer. Your agent isn't either.

You direct an AI agent to build your bitcoin app. It writes the code fast. But nobody on the team is a UX specialist, so nobody is checking how the app actually feels to the person using it.

In bitcoin that gap costs more than in most apps. People are moving real money, and some actions cannot be undone.

Where agents go wrong

7 in 10 builders saw their AI get it wrong.

We interviewed 10 bitcoin builders about how they really build with AI. 7 of the 10 had AI output that was made up, wrong or misaligned with what they asked for.

"Sometimes it just made up, hallucinated and I had to give it the documentation to see how it was supposed to build it."A bitcoin builder, from our research interviews

What it is

An open-source UX agent you give to your own agent.

An open-source UX agent, or skill pack, that you hand to the AI agent you already use. It carries a UX designer's judgment about how bitcoin apps should look and behave, so your agent can make better decisions without you becoming a designer.
